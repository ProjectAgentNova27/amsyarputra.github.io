Privacy

SITE INFORMATION

Overview

This website is a personal tech portal. It provides public links, discovery metadata, and links to selected private services.

Private services are protected through Cloudflare Access, VPN, or local authentication. Direct DDNS fallback links are intentionally not published here.

Data and logs

This site is hosted using GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Basic request metadata may be processed by those platforms for security, caching, analytics, abuse prevention, and service delivery.

The public status endpoint may check whether listed hostnames are reachable. It does not provide authenticated access to private services.

Browser Emulator

Emulator is publicly accessible without Cloudflare Access. Game and BIOS files are processed on your device, not uploaded or stored on the server. No games are supplied.

Progress checkpoints are stored in your browser, with seven-day retention and cleanup when a session starts. Reselect the same game to restore progress. Public and LAN URLs have separate storage. Clearing site data or browser storage eviction can remove saves; export saves for a permanent backup. Emulator assets may be cached, but selected game and BIOS files are not. Emulator settings, control mappings and cheats are also stored locally per game and remain until site data is cleared. They are not synced between devices or public/LAN hostnames. The simulated DS microphone does not record audio.

Mini Photo

Mini Photo edits photographs locally on compatible iPhone and iPad browsers. Photographs are not uploaded. Optional recovery stores one session on your device, with a 24-hour expiry checked when the app opens. Browser storage can be evicted. Presets and static app assets may persist locally. Exports omit embedded source EXIF and GPS; visible camera details or captions in a frame are chosen by you. Save / Share uses your device's system sheet where supported.

Mini Booth

Mini Booth is a public photobooth for compatible iPhone and iPad browsers and Home Screen web apps. Camera access starts only after your permission; no microphone is requested. Ordinary capture, editing and exports stay on your device. Photos remain in browser memory and can be lost on reload or browser termination.

Generating a download QR explicitly uploads an encrypted finished copy for up to 15 minutes. Its decryption key stays in the private link fragment, not on the server. Anyone with the complete link can access the copy before expiry; you can delete it sooner.

Remote shutter requires approval on the camera host. Control messages pass through the server; an optional low-resolution framing preview is encrypted between the devices and relayed without storage. Original photographs stay on the host. Pairings expire after 30 minutes and can be revoked. Keep pairing and download links private.

Amsyar Lab

Lab requires Cloudflare Access externally, or home-network/VPN access via its LAN hostname. Unlike the browser Emulator, Lab uploads selected files to temporary server storage for processing. Completed results expire five minutes after completion and can be deleted sooner. Restarting Lab discards jobs and sessions. Network diagnostic tools are currently disabled.

Portal preferences and Document Studio

The portal stores only your appearance preference in local browser storage. Search and category filtering stay in the current page; no analytics or tracking scripts are added by the portal.

Document Studio processes documents on your device. Recoverable sessions and optional saved projects use browser-managed storage, which can be evicted. Export backups for durable copies. Core editing/export works offline after caching; optional English OCR needs its local assets cached separately.
